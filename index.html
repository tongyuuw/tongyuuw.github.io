Tongyu (Tory) Wang | 王同宇

I am a senior undergraduate at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), majoring in Theoretical and Applied Mechanics with a minor in Computer Science. This summer I am a visiting researcher at Stanford's Biomimetics and Dexterous Manipulation Lab through the UGVR program.

Previously, I worked in the Soft Mechanics and Robotics Lab at USTC with Prof. Liu Wang, and visited the Physics of Fluids Lab at the University of Twente with Prof. Corinna Maass.

My research is in medical and continuum robotics, mechanism design, force sensing, physics-based simulation, and Sim2Real control. I am seeking PhD opportunities in robotics, mechanics, and related areas.

Research
DistalShift: How Distal Redundancy Shapes Robot Arm Motion and Joint Loading
Tian-Ao Ren*, Tongyu Wang*, Shixuan Zhao, Rohan Karunaratne, Tao Zhang, Rachel Thomasson, and Mark Cutkosky
Stanford Biomimetics and Dexterous Manipulation Lab, 2026
*equal contribution / project / code

A compact 2-DoF add-on wrist that localizes tool reorientation near the endpoint, so an existing robot arm can change probe orientation with less proximal motion and joint loading.

Air-Gecko: A Variable-stiffness Haptic Controller Using Positive-Pressure and Gecko-Inspired Interlayer Jamming for Continuum Manipulators
Tao Zhang*, Tian-Ao Ren*, Tongyu Wang, EmJ Rennich, Xinai Zhang, Shixuan Zhao, Yimeng Qin, Huxin Gao, Jiewen Lai, Hongliang Ren, and Mark Cutkosky
Stanford Biomimetics and Dexterous Manipulation Lab, 2026
*equal contribution / manuscript

An isomorphic haptic controller for medical continuum robots that uses positive pressure and gecko-inspired interlayer jamming to change stiffness during teleoperation.

Shape-Environment Fusion for Continuum-Robot Force Sensing
Tongyu Wang
Georgia Institute of Technology, Apr 2026 - present
Remote Research Assistant, BM2 Lab

A method for inferring distributed contact forces from robot shape and environmental constraints. The work combines Coulomb-friction contact in a Cosserat-rod model with constrained EKF/MAP estimation, fusing FBG shape sensing and vision in a closed-loop simulation and validation pipeline.

Sim2Real Control for Magnetic Robotic Intervention
Tongyu Wang
Soft Mechanics and Robotics Lab, USTC, Oct 2025 - present
Undergraduate Researcher, advised by Prof. Liu Wang

ROS/Gazebo workflows for a magnetic robotic arm, PRM* motion planning with Cartesian interpolation, and continuum-robot simulations in SOFA. Current work studies reinforcement-learning transfer from simulation to a physical system for vascular intervention.

Active Drops in Surfactant Systems
Tongyu Wang
Physics of Fluids Lab, University of Twente, Jul 2025 - Sep 2025
Visiting Research Student, advised by Prof. Corinna Maass / lab

High-Péclet-number transitions in active drops. I implemented multiphase CFD simulations in C++ with Basilisk and used Python to analyze results, evaluate governing equations, and verify reduced physical models.

Mechanics of Slope-Based Gravity Energy Storage
Tongyu Wang
Gravitas Energy, 2025.09 - 2026.02
R&D Intern

Unequal-height catenary models and numerical solvers for slack and sag in mountain-slope cyclic transmission systems. The work supports mechanical analysis and layout optimization for long-distance, high-load energy storage infrastructure, and led to the patent outputs below.
