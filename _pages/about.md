I am a junior undergraduate student at the **University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)**, majoring in **Mechanics** with a minor in **Computer Science**. My passion lies in the intersection of bio-inspired robotics, intelligent control, and advanced mechanism design. 

I am currently an applicant for summer research programs (UGVR) and future PhD positions. My goal is to develop next-generation robotic systems that possess both physical robustness and human-like perceptivity.

Research Interests
======
* **Bio-inspired Robotics:** Developing robots with high-maneuverability and multi-modal sensing inspired by biological systems.
* **Mechanism Design & Mechatronics:** Bridging theoretical mechanical analysis with high-fidelity hardware prototyping.
* **Intelligent Control:** Implementing perception-based control algorithms using ROS and computer vision.
* **Energy Storage Systems:** Exploring gravity-based energy storage solutions through innovative mechanism designs.
